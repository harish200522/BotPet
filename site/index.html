How it works

Three things, on a loop.

01

It watches the foreground

Every second it checks the window in front of you — the URL, title, and process. Everything stays local on your machine.

02

It respects your grace period

A quick glance at a video is fine. But when the timer expires, your companion gets up and marches over.

03

It closes the distraction

A browser tab gets closed via shortcut. You get your focus back, and your companion earns XP and returns to rest.

Affection & Levels

It remembers your focus milestones.

Every distraction closed earns XP, and so does patting your companion. Progress is tracked cleanly across your focus journey.

  • 25 XP per window or tab closed
  • 5 XP for each pat and interaction
  • Settings and rules are fully customizable

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it send data anywhere?

No. There is no telemetry, no tracking, and no external servers. Everything runs locally on your own machine.

Which animals are supported?

BotPet includes Classic Cat, Chibi Panda, and Playful Puppy.

Can I customize what gets closed?

Yes. You have full control over detection rules, grace periods, snooze durations, and a "Never touch" whitelist.

Can I just have the pet without automatic closing?

Yes! Simply toggle the mode to "Only complain" or turn "On patrol" off to enjoy a cute interactive companion.
